# FT-02 Current Battery Frozen Specification

Source: user-provided product specification image, 2026-09-25.

## Identified battery

- Product model: 955565 / 955465 (shared specification)
- Chemistry: Polymer lithium battery
- Nominal capacity: 5000 mAh
- Nominal voltage: 3.8 V (source also states “3.7 V compatible”; bottom table lists 3.7 V)
- Dimensions: 9.5 × 55 × 65 mm
- Weight: 76 g

## Charge specification stated by source

- Standard charge current: 0.5C = 2.5 A
- Fast charge current: 1.0C = 5 A
- Maximum charge current: 1C = 5 A
- Standard charge method stated by source: 0.5C CC to 4.25 V, then CV at 4.2 V until charge current <= 0.05C
- Charge termination current: <= 0.05C = <= 250 mA
- Charge time: 2–4 h standard; about 2 h fast

### Source ambiguity that must not be silently resolved

The source text says “CC charge to 4.25 V, then CV at 4.2 V”. This sequence is internally inconsistent because the CV voltage is lower than the preceding CC transition voltage. Do not use 4.25 V as an implementation target without clarification from the battery supplier/original datasheet.

## Discharge specification stated by source

- Maximum discharge current: 1C = 5 A
- Discharge cutoff voltage: 2.75 V
- Discharge temperature: -20 °C to +60 °C

- Overdischarge

## FT-02 PowerBoard implications

1. The current battery is capable, according to the supplied product specification, of 5 A maximum charge and 5 A maximum discharge.
2. FT-02 PowerBoard charging does not need to exploit 5 A. A 1.0 A default charge current remains intentionally conservative, and 1.5–2.0 A can remain board-capability options if system power and thermal budgets allow.
3. A charger limited to 2 A does not violate the battery's stated maximum charge current.
4. Battery power path, protection bypass strategy, shunt, connector and PCB copper should not assume more than 5 A continuous battery discharge unless a stronger source is obtained.
5. Future 10000 mAh compatibility remains a separate expansion target and must not change the current 5000 mAh frozen baseline.
